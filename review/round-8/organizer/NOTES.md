**Lite order.** The engine flattens the lists in file order, and the lite market keeps the first 40. The file is now written as vendors, walkers, queues, browsing, groups, benches, walkers_more. So the lite 40 are:
- 13 vendors (every stall has one);

- the Glühwein and Bier queues;
- one customer per deco counter;
- the Bücherstand browsers;

The round-8 parka figure is kept out of the lite 40, because the engine's `LITE_ALIAS` table doesn't map it.

**Side stalls: lite figures only (Mac's note).** The 8 deco vendors and their 10 customers (18 people) name the lite file as their model (`"model": "people_vendor_deco_m.lite.glb"`, `"lod": "lite"`). The engine loads and draws exactly that: `liteVariant()` of a `.lite.glb` finds no lighter file, so there is no full level and no LOD swap. The full `people_vendor_deco_m.glb` is never downloaded. The `variants` list now names only the files the crowd actually uses, because the full market preloads every variant. The customers use only figures whose lite file the lite market already loads, so they cost the lite market no extra download. The ornament shop vendor and customers stay full figures, because the shop is interactive and has a close view.

## 2. Nobody on the stroll path, nobody inside a stall

`blender/people/crowd_field.py` (new) builds 0.1 m distance fields from the same sources as the architect's stroll check:
- **Footprints:** every stall's, ride's, tree's and signpost's triangles in the body band (0.05–2.0 m). It also includes every vendor prop set on its slot, read from `props.json`. That means the deco crates at `slot_front`, the Bier barrel tables and the book cabinets all count as obstacles. `blender/people/dump_tris.mjs` (new) reads them.
- **Furniture and poles:** `furniture.json` and `architect_plan.POLES_THREE`.
- **Stroll legs:** all 36 legs of `layout.json` `stroll.legs`, on the centripetal Catmull-Rom the engine walks.
- **The plaza edge.**

Rules for people who stand still:
- ≥ 1.0 m from every leg (the architect's check fails at 0.6 m and warns below 0.95 m);
- ≥ 0.35 m from any stall or prop surface;
- ≥ 0.45 m from furniture;
- 0.55 m from each other;
- outside the near wedge and the corridor of every stop's view.

Groups search outward for a free spot. Queues and counter customers search the nearest free cell in the stall's frame.

**Walkers** are planned with A* on a 0.2 m grid. The cost function:
- blocks stalls, furniture and standing people;
- adds a penalty within 0.8 m (strong) and 1.1 m (light) of the loop line;
- adds a penalty for walking through a stop's view.

Paths are then string-pulled. Each companion walks on whichever side of its partner's path is clear.

**Checks, re-run at the end against the architect's `layout.json` (unchanged since 03:38; the regenerated crowd.json is byte-identical):**
- `crowd_plan.py`: 0 problems. Nobody stands inside a stall, within 1.0 m of a leg, or on another person. No walker crosses a stall or a standing person. Every clip exists in its file, and every mug flag matches its clip.
- The architect's own check (`blender/square/stroll.py`, CHECK_ONLY), run through `blender/people/check_stroll.py`, which redirects its map and report to a scratch folder so nothing is written into the architect's folders: **0 failures on all 36 legs, no person WARN.** The nearest standing person to any leg is 1.25 m (the Käse customer, on Christbaumschmuck → Karussell).
- `review/round-8/organizer/crowd_topdown.jpg` is the top-down placement check. It shows the field, both kinds of leg with their 1.0 m keep-out band, the stop views, every person, the walker paths, and the lite-only people ringed.

## Open issues and notes for other roles

- **Engineer:** crowd.json now relies on the existing behaviour that a `.lite.glb` model is drawn as-is, with no LOD level. A `lod: "lite"` field documents it. Full-market cap: the engine slices the full crowd at 90, and the file has 87.
- **Walkers cross stroll legs.** Crossing can't be avoided where lanes meet, and the front of the square is crossed by most signpost legs. Walkers keep about 0.8 m or more off the loop line where they run alongside it. They don't yield to the stroll camera. A short pause or sidestep when the camera is within about 1.5 m would be an engine change.
- **The Bier queue** has only 1 person at the counter. The other 2 wait just beyond the barrel tables on the stall's front right (local x 2.4–3.0, z 2.95), because the front of the counter is the Bier view and the props fill its right end.
- **Lite-only deco people** read softer than full figures at 2 m: no ears, simpler hands. The deco vendors keep their faces through `lite_face`. If the browser view shows the customers' backs as too coarse, the change is one line in `crowd_plan.py` (`lite_only=False` for customers).
- `people_anims.glb` was not rebuilt, as in round 6. Figure files carry their own clips, so nothing plays it for vendors.
- Re-run `python3 blender/people/crowd_plan.py` and then `python3 blender/people/check_stroll.py` after any layout or stroll change.

# Organizer, round 1: people, crowd and choreography

## What was built

- **16 skinned figures**, each with a `.lite.glb`, in `site/public/models/`. All are modelled, rigged and animated in code in `blender/people/`, with no third-party assets.
  - **8 crowd figures:**
    - `people_man_coat`: long double-breasted coat and flat cap.
    - `people_woman_coat`: belted camel coat and bobble hat.
    - `people_man_parka`: hooded parka with fur trim, beard and beanie.
    - `people_woman_older`: glasses, beret and handbag.
    - `people_man_older`: bald, glasses and trilby.
    - `people_woman_young`: quilted jacket, long striped scarf and snow boots.
    - `people_child_boy`: 1.18 m, puffer and earflap hat.
    - `people_child_girl`: 1.28 m, duffle coat and pigtails.
  - **4 musicians** (`people_band_sax`, `_piano`, `_bass`, `_drums`). Their hands are solved with IK against the ride builder's instrument geometry (`blender/rides/instruments.py`), so they meet the instruments at `slot_sax`, `slot_piano`, `slot_bass` and `slot_drums`:
    - the pianist's hands are on the keys;
    - the saxophonist's mouth is at the mouthpiece;
    - the bassist has his left hand on the neck and his right plucking;
    - the drummer is seated with a brush in each hand, on snare and ride.
  - **4 vendors**, one per section stall, each with an apron:
    - `people_vendor_gluehwein`
    - `people_vendor_bier` (hat with a feather)
    - `people_vendor_wurst`
    - `people_vendor_buecher` (glasses and beard)
- **Materials.** Every figure has the materials `coat`, `scarf`, `hat`, `body` and `mug`.
  - `coat`, `scarf` and `hat` are double-sided and carry procedural knit or wool normal maps. Their base colour is the material factor × COLOR_0, so the engine can recolour a person by setting `material.color` on those three materials (tested in three.js).
  - `body` holds skin, hair, trousers, boots, gloves and apron as vertex colour.
- **Clips** (24 fps, loops, in place):
  - crowd: `idle`, `walk` (1.2 s cycle), `chat`, `drink`, `laugh`, `sit`, plus `*_free` twins (`idle_free`, `walk_free`, `chat_free`, `laugh_free`, `sit_free`) that hide the Glühwein mug by scaling the `mug` bone to 0.001. With mug or without, the clip alone decides.
  - band figures add `play` (8 s slow loop) and `rest`.
  - vendors add `serve` and `wipe`.
  - All figures have all crowd clips.
- **`site/src/crowd.json`** is generated by `blender/people/crowd_plan.py` from `site/src/layout.json` and the stalls' `slot_vendor` and `slot_front` empties. It holds 90 people plus 4 musicians:
  - 4 vendors at `slot_vendor` (world position including y and rotY, clip `serve`, idle clip `wipe`);
  - 3 queues with 12 people: Glühwein 5, Bier 4, Riesenrad ticket booth 3;
  - 10 walkers plus 2 in `walkers_more`, on paths along the aisles with speed and start phase;
  - 62 people in groups of 2 to 5, placed:
    - at the stalls and deco stalls, browsing;
    - around the tree, including a family;
    - listening at the bandstand;
    - a couple and a single on the benches (`sit` at seat height 0.475);
    - children at the Karussell.
  - Each person has `variant` (index into `variants`), `model`, `clip`, `mug`, `colors` {coat, scarf, hat} and `phase`.
  - The file is ordered most important first; the lite market keeps the first 40.
  - All positions are checked against stalls, the bandstand, the tree, the Karussell, the Riesenrad, lamp poles and benches (`crowd_map.jpg`).
- **Previews** (Cycles, 2 threads, 48 samples, OIDN, 1280x720):
  - `lineup.jpg`: the 8 crowd figures in front, vendors and band behind;
  - `band.jpg`: the four musicians posed at their slots on the real bandstand with the real instruments, `play` at t = 1 s;
  - `group_chat.jpg`: four adults chatting and drinking, with a child;
  - `crowd_map.jpg`: top-down plan of crowd.json over the layout.

## Rebuild

```
/home/claude/tools/bpy-venv/bin/python blender/people/build.py [--only people_man_coat,...] [--no-lite]
/home/claude/tools/bpy-venv/bin/python blender/people/crowd_plan.py        # after layout.json changes
/home/claude/tools/bpy-venv/bin/python blender/people/preview.py lineup|band|group --samples 48
```

- Each export runs: Blender glTF export (actions, NLA) → `slim_anims.mjs` (resample, drop channels that stay at rest) → the carpenter's `blender/lib/optimize.mjs` (meshopt and WebP; textures 256 for full, 128 for lite).
- `preview.py` imports the raw, non-meshopt glbs from `blender/out/raw/` and `blender/out/people/raw/`, because Blender's importer cannot read meshopt.

## Hand-off requests to the engineer (site/src/crowd.js, engine/instruments.js)

The engine already loads `variants`, places groups, queues and walkers, and plays a walk or idle clip. To use the rest of crowd.json:

1. Play `person.clip` by name, with `mixer.setTime(phase)`, instead of the regex pick. Today every standing person plays `idle`, so every mug shows.
2. Apply `colors`: clone the `coat`, `scarf` and `hat` materials per person (or per colour set) and set `.color`.
3. Vendors: the entry has a world `pos` [x, y, z] with y = 0.13 (the stall floor) and `rotY`. crowd.js forces y = 0, so vendors stand 13 cm too low, inside the floor boards.
4. Band: `engine/instruments.js` still puts `buildPerson` stand-ins at the bandstand slots. Please load `people_band_<inst>.glb` at the slot's world transform, giving it exactly the same world transform as the instrument on that slot (the slot's position and yaw; the pose is solved in the instrument's frame), and play `play`. The `musicians` entries in crowd.json carry slot, model, clip and seated. `act_sax` sway and `act_brush` sweep then need to move the player with the instrument, or be switched off, or the hands drift off the keys and brushes.
5. `walk` is in place. Move the root along the path at `speed` (about 1.0 m/s adult, 0.8 m/s child) so the feet do not skate.
6. Draw calls: each person is 2 meshes with 5 materials, so about 5 draw calls; 90 people means about 450. If that is too many, merge per variant, or instance by variant with a shared mixer per clip and phase bucket.

## Contract notes

- The engine caps the crowd at 90 (full) and 40 (lite), so crowd.json holds exactly 90 people. The brief's groups, walkers, queues, bench couple and kids all fit, but it is thinner than the 130 or so the first plan had.
- Vendor y = 0.13 as above.
- The 4 musicians are listed without a position (slot only), so the engine's parser skips them and they do not use crowd places.
- I wrote nothing outside my paths. The bandstand and instrument geometry is read-only input from `blender/rides/` and `blender/out/raw/`.

## Open issues and what to improve next

- **Lite file size** is about 0.82 of full, not a third. Next step: move the clips into one shared `people_anims.glb`, rotations only, retargeted by bone name in three.js; the skeletons share topology. By estimate, each figure would then drop to roughly the mesh and texture share (about 60 kB full, 40 kB lite).
- **Faces** are simple (nose wedge, eyes and brows by vertex colour). Beanies read a little helmet-like at close range, and there is no AO bake on the figures.
- In `group_chat.jpg` one gesture hand grazes the neighbour's coat. Group spacing is a little tight for the `chat` gestures.
- `slot_vendor` sits about 1 m behind the counter, so the `serve` reach toward the counter is approximate.
- Child `sit` without a bench reads as a crouch; use it only on benches (crowd.json does).
- Two walker paths pass about 0.2 m from a lamp pole (reported by `crowd_plan.py`).
- The instruments' own animation (the engine's `act_sax` and `act_brush`) is not synced to the player clips.
- `gltf-transform inspect` reports a bbox of ±1 for the scene, an artefact of KHR_mesh_quantization's node scale. The real height is 1.18 to 1.86 m.

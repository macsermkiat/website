# Glossary

**Market**: the whole scene: the square, its stalls, the rides, the bandstand, the town around it and the people in it. It is the homepage.

**Section stall**: a stall that opens a section of the site when a visitor clicks it. There are four:
- Glühwein stand: About
- Bierstand: Projects
- Bratwurst stand: Writing
- Bücherstand (bookshop): Reading

**Landmark**: a clickable place that is not a stall.
- Bandstand: Music
- Riesenrad (Ferris wheel): Big questions
- Karussell (carousel): Contact

**Deco stall**: a stall that is lit, stocked and part of the scenery but opens nothing (Lebkuchen, roasted almonds, candles, toys and so on).

**Panel**: the card that opens over the market when a visitor enters a section stall or landmark. It holds the section's content and its actions.

**Action**: something a visitor can do inside a panel that changes the scene, such as pouring a mug, pulling a pint or pulling a book.

**Ride**: an action that moves the visitor's view with a landmark: sitting in a Ferris wheel gondola or on a carousel horse.

**Band**: the jazz quartet on the bandstand: tenor sax, piano, double bass and drums played with brushes. It plays ballads: slow, sad, warm.

**Crowd**: the people in the market, standing in groups or walking the lanes.

**Snow**: falling snow and snow cover. The visitor can turn it on and off.

**Lite market**: the lighter version of the market served to phones and weak devices, with the same sections and content.

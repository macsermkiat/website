A guided stroll: choose a place on the signpost (or press 1–8) and walk there; the arrows walk on to the next stall. Every stall's words are written in it: on the Glühwein chalkboard, the Bierdeckel, the Bratwurst menu, the pages of a book, the sheet music, the Riesenrad noticeboard and the Karussell ticket. Click them, or press Enter, to read. At the Bücherstand, tap a cabinet and then a book. The ornament shop is for play: ring the baubles and look for the pickle. Sound starts only when you press play.

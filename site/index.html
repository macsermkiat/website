How to visit

  • Pick a place on the signpost, or press 1 to 8. The arrows walk you to the next stall.
  • Each stall's words are written on something in it: a chalkboard, a beer mat, a menu, a book, sheet music, a noticeboard, a ticket. Click it, or press Enter, to read.
  • At the Bücherstand, tap a cabinet, then a book.
  • The ornament shop is for play: light the candle arch, brush the glass baubles, look into the mirror ball.
  • Sound starts only when you press play.
